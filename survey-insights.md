## Google Form Survey Insights

This file contains summary statistics collected from the Google Form survey for the Nutrify project.

### Survey Overview

The survey was conducted to understand user's food habits, nutrition awareness, health goals and expectations from a nutrition-based platform. It helped us analyze people's lifestyle and assess how aware they are of maintaining proper dietary intake according to their health conditions. 

We also gathered information about the functionalities users expect from such a platform, which helped us better understand user needs and design a more effective and user-friendly solution.

### Total Responses

## Key Findings

- Most users are interested in personalized nutrition recommendations.
- Many users want diet plans based on their health goals.
- Users prefer a simple and easy-to-use platform.
- The survey shows good potential demand for the Nutrify project.

## Conclusion

The survey results helped us understand user needs and validate the idea behind Nutrify. Based on the responses, Nutrify can focus on personalized diet plans, health goal tracking, and simple nutrition guidance.
